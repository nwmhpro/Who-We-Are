The Father

Jesus said the Father in me doeth the work. The Father in him toils. The scriptures says God was in Christ reconciling the world to Himself. Now this takes on a different meaning. How do we then separate Jesus from God? This totally eradicates the thought of an angry God vs. a passive loving Jesus. This goes on to show it is our minds and hearts that have to be renewed to who God really is.

 

Again Jesus says the Father who dwells in me does the work. Dwells means stay. Jesus is saying the Father stays in me. So the question is. When Jesus prayed was He praying without or praying within?

 

Jesus said before the Roman soldiers came, yet I'm not alone because the Father is with me. We were taught God is in the sky, not within. We were taught to look outside ourselves. Was that teaching correct?

 

When you read Jesus then says I go to the Father. Okay I perceive the duality of God above and God within. What we do is choose one way versus embracing both as Jesus said and did. We need to grow in the fullness of the word. Because if the Father was doing the work in Jesus and we shall do greater work, who then is working in us? The Father in us should be the one doing the work also.

 

"On that day you will realize that I am in my Father, and you are in me, and I am in you." - John 14:20
